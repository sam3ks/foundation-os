# Foundation OS

Foundation OS is an open-source platform designed to help foundations, trusts, NGOs, associations, and community organizations manage and grow their impact.

The goal is simple:

> **Enable communities to organize, collaborate, and create measurable social impact.**

## Why Foundation OS?

Many community organizations rely on spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups, and manual processes to manage members, volunteers, initiatives, donations, and events.

Foundation OS aims to provide a unified platform that helps communities:

- Manage memberships
- Organize volunteers
- Run initiatives and events
- Track donations and impact
- Improve transparency and accountability
- Engage members at grassroots level

## Vision

To become the operating system for community-led development.

A platform where every individual can contribute, every initiative can be tracked, and every impact can be measured.

## Core Principles

### Community First

People drive change. Technology enables it.

### Transparency

Activities, contributions, and outcomes should be visible and measurable.

### Grassroots Participation

Anyone should be able to participate, regardless of technical background.

### Governance

Communities should be empowered while following defined processes and standards.

### Inclusivity

The platform should support multilingual communities and diverse forms of participation.

## Initial Areas of Focus

- Membership Management
- Volunteer Management
- Initiative Management
- Event Management
- Donation Tracking
- Workflow & Approvals
- Impact Reporting
- Community Engagement

## Status

This project is currently in the foundation and discovery phase.

The initial focus is on defining the operating model, governance framework, and core capabilities before implementation.
